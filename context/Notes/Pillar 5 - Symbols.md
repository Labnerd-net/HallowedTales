## Angel iconography and classification

(Folded in from the retired Pillar 6 angels proposal, 2026-10-07 — these read as the "what it
means, how usage developed" explainer shape this pillar already uses, not a legend-with-
retellings shape.)

- **Archangel iconographic attributes** — Michael's sword/scales, Gabriel's lily/trumpet,
  Raphael's staff and fish (from Tobit); how these let an artwork identify which archangel is
  depicted.
- **Guardian angel iconography** — why guardian angels are conventionally painted guiding
  children across bridges or dangerous terrain, and when that convention emerged.
- **The nine choirs of angels (per Pseudo-Dionysius) and why only three angels (Michael, Gabriel,
  Raphael) are given personal names in scripture** — reference/explainer piece on how Catholic
  tradition organizes and names angels.

## Research notes

- Each entry eventually wants: earliest known written source, rough date, a one-line "what we
  actually know historically" contrast, and - distinct from the legends pillar - a short
  `meaning` line plus, where there's enough material, a `usageTimeline` of how the symbol's use
  changed across eras (see the Chi-Rho source pass for a worked example: catacombs -> labarum ->
  sarcophagi -> Paschal candle -> heraldry).
- Devotional art origin stories (the tilma, the Black Madonna, Divine Mercy, Veronica's Veil)
  stay in Pillar 1 for now - those are each a single historical image/icon tied to one
  apparition or artifact, closer to a legend-plus-artifact shape than a recurring iconographic
  mark. Revisit if that line starts feeling arbitrary once more symbol entries are drafted.
